# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners and managers of small and medium businesses registered in Krasnodar Krai (ИП and legal entities, SME status, on the market more than a year) deciding whether to enter the regional stage of the competition and where to find the application form, deadlines and documents.
Secondary, all year round: partners and sponsors (banks, media, business associations), press, past winners and laureates looking for results, ceremony reports and photos.

## Product Purpose

Website of the regional stage of the National award for entrepreneurship «Золотой Меркурий» (established by ТПП России in 2002, supported by the Federation Council and the State Duma), run by Союз «Торгово-промышленная палата Краснодарского края». The site must get eligible businesses to apply during the intake window and, outside it, show the award's weight: winners, ceremony, partners, news.

## Positioning

The only official regional gateway to «Золотой Меркурий» in Krasnodar Krai: participation is free, regional winners go on to the federal stage. Regional expert council made of the chamber and business-support institutions.

## Operating Context

Yearly cycle: 15 January — intake opens; 15 March — intake closes; by 28 March — expert council evaluation; April — award ceremony in Krasnodar. Outside the window the site shows «Прием заявок окончен» plus results. Since 2017 (eighth year in 2024; 187 applications in 2026, a record).
13 nominations in three groups: Лучшее предприятие МСП (9 sectors), Лучшее предприятие-экспортёр (3 sectors), Лучшее семейное предприятие. Three application forms, one per group.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on reg.ru shared hosting (Apache + PHP, no Node at runtime); application form posts to a PHP mailer.
- Russian language only.
- Content on hand: about-text, prizes, eligibility criteria, nominations, stages, FAQ (7 questions), partners (general / official / media), 34 news posts 2021–2026 with photos, contacts.
- Undecided: whether the redesign will be seasonal (two hero states) or one hero for both; this variant catalog explores both.

## Brand Commitments

- Name «Золотой Меркурий», «Региональный конкурс», «Национальная премия в области предпринимательской деятельности».
- Brand = colors + logo, not the old layout: yellow-gold #f6eb55, black, light grey #ededed; the medal with Mercury; the ТПП Краснодарского края logo.
- Owner taste: rejects strict corporate «site from 2015»; wants modern, bold, premium.

## Evidence on Hand

- Ceremony and partner photos from news posts (permitted for use): goldmercury93/site-tilda/images in the tilda-migration project.
- Real figures: 187 applications (2026), ~150 (2024), 30 laureates and winners (2021), 13 nominations, since 2002 federally.
- No testimonials or winner quotes on hand; do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Deadline and the single next action are always obvious in season.
2. Show the award's weight with real people and real numbers, never invented claims.
3. Free participation and the path to the federal stage are the strongest arguments.
4. Partners are visible but never outrank participants.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast; dark text on yellow (white on #f6eb55 is 1.24:1 and is forbidden).
